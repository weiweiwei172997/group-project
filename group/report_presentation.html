  • 新闻文本只保留和股票相关的条目，避免噪声污染情绪信号。

04 / RL
Member 4State & DQN

The agent sees price, position, cash, and language at the same time.

State vector
close MA50 MA200 RSI MACD position cash sentiment_score
reward = log(equity_t+1 / equity_t) - risk_penalty × |asset_return| × exposure
  • Actions: Hold / Buy / Sell.
  • Double DQN + target network + replay buffer.
  • Walk-forward split avoids look-ahead bias.
Why this formulation works
  • Technical indicators capture momentum and trend.
  • cash and position prevent unrealistic inventory assumptions.
  • sentiment_score lets the policy react to information flow before price fully moves.
Demo note: If asked why DQN is needed, answer: it learns sequential decisions, not one-step classification.

07B / Interpretation
Member 6Answer the hard question early

Why are some returns still weak, even when NLP helps?

Three structural reasons
  • Weak market window: even buy-and-hold has a negative average cumulative return.
  • Sparse news flow: about 81.5% of trading days have zero matched news items.
  • Trading friction: transaction cost and risk penalty deliberately suppress over-trading.
So the system is improving decision quality, not claiming guaranteed profitability.
What we should claim in the report
  • Do say: “NLP improves risk-adjusted performance.”
  • Do say: “With NLP beats the no-NLP baseline in our ablation.”
  • Do not say: “The strategy always makes money.”
  • Do not hide the negative-return folds; explain them.
